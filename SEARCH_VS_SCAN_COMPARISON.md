## 💡 **WHY THIS STRATEGY WORKS**

### **Psychological Triggers:**

1. **Free Trial Effect**
   - Users experience premium quality
   - No risk, no commitment
   - Builds trust

2. **Loss Aversion**
   - Users get premium features
   - Then lose them when scanning
   - Creates desire to get them back

3. **Consistency Principle**
   - Used AI in search
   - Want same experience scanning
   - Natural upgrade path

4. **Social Proof**
   - "Everyone searching gets premium"
   - "Join premium for unlimited access"
   - FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)
