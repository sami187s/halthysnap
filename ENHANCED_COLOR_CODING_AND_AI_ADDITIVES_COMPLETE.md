## 🎨 **Enhanced Color-Coded Ingredients**

### **NEW COLOR SCHEME:**
- **🟢 Excellent** → Dark Green (`#1B5E20`) - Premium, highly beneficial ingredients
- **🟢 Good** → Regular Green (`#4CAF50`) - Safe and beneficial ingredients
