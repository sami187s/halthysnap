# Cosmetic Ingredient Issues - FIXED ✅

## Issues Fixed

### 1. ✅ **Changed "Moderate" to "Average"**
- Updated all UI text from "Moderate" to "Average" 
- Changed in ingredient counts summary
- Updated in legend display
- Modified in individual ingredient status
- Updated score descriptions

### 2. ✅ **Improved Ingredient Descriptions**
**Problem**: Ingredients showed generic text like "safe and beneficial ingredient" instead of explaining WHY they're rated that way.

**Solution**: Added comprehensive, informative descriptions that explain:

#### **Excellent Ingredients** - Why they're so good:
- **Aqua/Water**: "Essential base ingredient - completely safe and necessary for product stability"
- **Glycerin**: "Natural humectant that draws moisture to skin - proven safe and effective"
- **Niacinamide**: "Form of Vitamin B3 that reduces oil production and minimizes pores - clinically proven"
- **Hyaluronic Acid**: "Powerful moisture-binding molecule - holds 1000x its weight in water"

#### **Good Ingredients** - Benefits with minor considerations:
- **Dimethicone**: "Silicone that creates protective barrier - safe but may cause buildup"
- **Retinol**: "Vitamin A that fights aging - very effective but start slowly to avoid irritation"
- **Glycolic Acid**: "AHA that exfoliates dead skin - effective for texture but requires sunscreen"

#### **Average Ingredients** - Explain the concerns:
- **Fragrance**: "Adds pleasant scent but can trigger allergies - patch test if sensitive"
- **Phenoxyethanol**: "Preservative that prevents bacteria growth - generally safe in low concentrations"
- **Alcohol**: "Can be drying and irritating - better in leave-on products than cleansers"

#### **Poor Ingredients** - Explain the risks:
- **Sodium Lauryl Sulfate**: "Harsh surfactant that strips skin - can cause dryness and irritation"
- **Parabens**: "Preservative with potential hormone-disrupting effects - avoid if possible"

## User Experience Improvements

### Before:
