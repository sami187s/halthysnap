## 🔬 DETAILED ADDITIVE INFORMATION NOW INCLUDES:

### 1. **Additive Identification**
- **Name**: Full chemical or common name
- **E-Number**: European food additive number (when applicable)
- **Count Badge**: Shows total number of additives found

### 2. **Safety Assessment**
- **Color-Coded Badges**: 
  - 🟢 Green = Excellent/Good (safe)
  - 🟠 Orange = Moderate (caution)
  - 🔴 Red = Poor (concerning)
- **Safety Score**: Overall 0-100 additive safety rating

### 3. **Functional Information**
- **Function Type**: Preservative, emulsifier, stabilizer, etc.
- **Health Impact**: AI-generated explanation of potential effects
- **Usage Context**: Why this additive is used in the product

### 4. **Natural Alternatives**
- **Alternative Suggestions**: Natural substitutes when available
- **Green Highlighting**: Eco-friendly presentation
- **Educational Value**: Helps users understand better options

### 5. **Summary & Recommendations**
- **AI Summary**: Overall assessment of all additives
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Actionable advice
- **Checkmark Icons**: Professional bullet-point presentation
